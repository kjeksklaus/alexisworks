Privacy Policy

PHEV.ev — Effective Date: 12 November 2026

PHEV.ev calculates charging efficiency for an electric or plug-in hybrid vehicle from the figures you enter. We do not run a cloud account service for the app, and we do not sell your data.

1. Data Collection

No accounts: PHEV.ev does not require an AlexisWorks account, email signup, or cloud login.

No analytics or advertising: We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking pixels. Charts in the app are calculations on your device, not usage tracking.

No sale of data: We do not sell, rent, or broker personal information.

2. Local Processing and Storage

Energy, charging speed, tariff, how often you charge, idle time, and equipment cost — and the results the app calculates from them — are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes that local data.

PHEV.ev does not upload those figures or results to AlexisWorks servers. It does not connect to your vehicle, charger, or energy supplier.

3. Permissions

The calculation does not need location, contacts, photos, calendar, or network access. The operating system is not asked for those permissions to run the app.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when features change. Updates will be posted on this page and, where practical, noted in the app.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy: dev@alexisworks.co.uk
