Privacy Policy

Biotic Waves — Last Updated: June 9, 2026

Biotic Waves is built with a strict philosophy of data minimization and digital autonomy. We believe your biological focus and neural data belong entirely to you.

1. Data Collection and Processing

Zero Personal Data: Biotic Waves does not collect, request, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). There are no account creations, names, email addresses, or phone numbers required to use the application.

100% Local Frequency Engine: All sound synthesis, frequency configurations, and session timers run completely on your local device. No audio signals, usage metadata, or slider settings ever leave your device.

Zero Third-Party Tracking: We do not use third-party analytics frameworks, advertising SDKs, or tracking pixels.

2. Data Storage

Any configuration state or preferences you save within the application are stored strictly within your device’s secure local storage (e.g., Apple Keychain or App Sandbox). Deleting the application completely wipes this data from existence.

3. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy to reflect future local feature iterations. Because we do not collect your contact details, updates will be posted directly within the application or on our official website.

4. Contact Us

For questions regarding this uncompromising privacy architecture, contact us at: dev@alexisworks.co.uk
