Privacy Policy

Dockless — Effective Date: August 11, 2026

Dockless is built for local control of your own Mac. We do not run a cloud account service for the app, and we do not sell your data.

1. Data Collection

No accounts: Dockless does not require an AlexisWorks account, email signup, or cloud login.

No analytics or advertising: We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking pixels.

No sale of data: We do not sell, rent, or broker personal information.

2. Local Processing and Storage

Launcher slots, themes, appearance preferences, and onboarding state are stored on your devices (for example, in local app storage or system preferences). Deleting the app removes that local data.

Dockless does not upload your launcher layout or preference data to AlexisWorks servers.

3. Device-to-Device Connection

Dockless connects your phone and Mac over your local network using Apple’s Multipeer Connectivity framework. Session traffic uses encryption provided by that framework. Peer discovery and control stay between your devices; they are not routed through AlexisWorks.

Your device may show a local network permission prompt so nearby Dockless peers can find each other. Granting or denying that permission is under your control in system settings.

4. Optional Network Requests

If you add a website launcher slot, Dockless may fetch a favicon for that site from public icon services so the tile can display an icon. That request uses the website host you configured. It is not used to build advertising profiles.

Opening a website or app from Dockless uses the browser or application on your Mac under your own accounts and settings.

5. Permissions

Depending on how you use Dockless, macOS or iOS may ask for permissions such as Local Network access or Login Items (if you enable launch at login). These permissions are managed by the operating system. You can change or revoke them at any time in System Settings.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when features change. Updates will be posted on this page and, where practical, noted in the app.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: dev@alexisworks.co.uk
