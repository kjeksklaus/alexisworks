Privacy Policy

iDock — Effective Date: 29 September 2026

iDock is a personal Mac desktop for the web apps you already use. We do not run a cloud account service for the app, and we do not sell your data.

1. Data Collection

No accounts: iDock does not require an AlexisWorks account, email signup, or cloud login.

No analytics or advertising: We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking pixels.

No sale of data: We do not sell, rent, or broker personal information.

2. Local Processing and Storage

Browsing history, search-engine choice, cookie policy, portal shortcuts, and appearance preferences are stored on your Mac, in the app’s local Application Support folder. Deleting the app removes that local data.

iDock does not upload your history, shortcuts, or preferences to AlexisWorks servers.

3. Calendar

If you use the calendar, iDock asks macOS for Calendar access through EventKit so it can show and edit the calendars already on this Mac. Event titles, times, locations, and notes stay on your device and with the calendar accounts you have already configured in macOS. They are not sent to AlexisWorks.

You can deny or revoke Calendar access at any time in System Settings.

4. Browsing and Third-Party Sites

iDock includes a browser for Apple and Google web apps, portal shortcuts you add, and web search. Pages you open are loaded from those sites. Sign-in cookies and site data stay in the local web view on your Mac. iDock does not operate Apple, Google, or any other site you visit.

Search uses the engine you choose. The default is Brave. You can also choose Startpage, DuckDuckGo, or Google. Your search query is sent to that engine under its own privacy policy. It is not sent to AlexisWorks.

History retention is under your control, including turning history off. You can clear history and cookies in the app.

5. Permissions

macOS may ask for Calendar access if you open the calendar. Network access is used to load the sites and search engines you choose. These permissions are managed by the operating system. You can change or revoke Calendar access at any time in System Settings.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when features change. Updates will be posted on this page and, where practical, noted in the app.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: dev@alexisworks.co.uk
