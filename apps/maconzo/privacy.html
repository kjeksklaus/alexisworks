Privacy Policy

Maconzo — Effective Date: June 7, 2026

At Maconzo, your privacy is our priority. We designed this application with a “privacy-first” approach.

No Data Collection or Storage

Maconzo does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any of your personal or financial data.

Local Processing

Maconzo operates as a direct interface. It reads your Google Sheet data in real-time and processes it locally on your machine. No data is ever saved on our servers or transmitted to any third-party databases.

Google Authentication

We use standard Google OAuth to securely access your data. We do not have access to your Google account password.

User Control

You maintain total control over your data access at all times.
